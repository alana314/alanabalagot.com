About the Song

You Can't Erase Us is a collaborative anthem celebrating the Trans Chorus of Los Angeles and the broader trans community. Released on International Transgender Day of Visibility in March 2026, the track weaves together voices of resilience and joy into a powerful choral statement.

The music video was filmed at the historic Connie Norman Transgender Empowerment Center in West Hollywood — a fitting backdrop for a song about visibility and belonging.

"“Pride is more than just a celebration. It started as a protest, and this is a time when we need to show our strength. I wrote this song because they're trying to erase us and our history. I can't think of better partners than Lambda Legal, who fights for our civil rights, and the Trans Chorus of Los Angeles, who show strength and visibility through art."
— Alana Balagot
